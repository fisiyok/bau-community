# SE-310 Software Engineering

Bay Atlantic University · Fall 2026

**Instructor:** Prof. Nina Patel  
**Meeting time:** Tuesday and Thursday, 1:30 PM  
**Location:** Tech Center 218

## Course overview

An applied introduction to the software development lifecycle, requirements, architecture, testing, collaboration, and iterative delivery. Teams will plan and build a small software product throughout the semester.

## Assessment

- Team project and milestones: 40%
- Individual technical exercises: 25%
- Design and testing reviews: 20%
- Participation and retrospectives: 15%

## Course expectations

Contribute consistently to your project team, document decisions, participate in code and design reviews, and communicate blockers promptly. Project work should be attributable and accessible to every team member.

## Tentative schedule

- Weeks 1–3: Requirements, user stories, and team formation
- Weeks 4–6: Architecture, interfaces, and sprint planning
- Weeks 7–10: Implementation, testing, and review
- Weeks 11–14: Delivery, retrospective, and presentations

Office hours: Wednesday, 11:00 AM–1:00 PM, Tech Center 220. Project milestones are posted in course announcements.
