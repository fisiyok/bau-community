# CS-301 Data Structures

Bay Atlantic University · Fall 2026

**Instructor:** Dr. Marcus Chen  
**Meeting time:** Monday and Wednesday, 10:00 AM  
**Location:** Engineering 402

## Course overview

Design and analysis of fundamental data structures and algorithms. Topics include lists, stacks, queues, trees, hash tables, graphs, sorting, and algorithmic complexity.

## Assessment

- Programming assignments: 30%
- Midterm examination: 25%
- Final examination: 30%
- Participation and problem sets: 15%

## Course expectations

Attend class prepared, submit original work by the posted deadlines, and ask for help early. Students may discuss approaches together but must write and explain their own submitted solutions.

## Tentative schedule

- Weeks 1–3: Complexity, lists, stacks, and queues
- Weeks 4–6: Trees, heaps, and hash tables
- Weeks 7–9: Graph representations and traversal
- Weeks 10–12: Sorting, searching, and review

Office hours: Tuesday, 2:00–4:00 PM, Engineering 410. Dates and assignment details are maintained in the course announcements.
