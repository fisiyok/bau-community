# BUS-205 Business Communication

Bay Atlantic University · Fall 2026

**Instructor:** Dr. Elaine Brooks  
**Meeting time:** Monday and Wednesday, 2:00 PM  
**Location:** Founders Hall 106

## Course overview

Practice clear, audience-aware communication in professional settings. The course covers written messages, presentations, research, collaboration, and communication across teams and cultures.

## Assessment

- Individual writing assignments: 30%
- Presentations: 25%
- Team case study: 25%
- Participation and peer feedback: 20%

## Course expectations

Bring drafts and questions to workshops, give specific and constructive peer feedback, and cite sources consistently. Written work should be clear, well organized, and appropriate to its audience.

## Tentative schedule

- Weeks 1–3: Audience, purpose, and professional writing
- Weeks 4–6: Research, proposals, and visual communication
- Weeks 7–10: Presentations and team case analysis
- Weeks 11–14: Portfolio revision and final presentations
